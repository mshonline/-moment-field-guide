# What got decided

## The workflow is three chunks

Only the first happens at the end of the day.

1. End of day. Phone library and SD card into the primary sessions drive, batch rename, drag the whole session folder to the backup sessions drive, check, then clear the phone and the card.
2. Editing. Rate, cull the no-goes, tone, export with both recipes (TIFF and Original), copyright pass and Finder rename, ChronoSync the whole drive.
3. Archive. When the session is done: drag the trimmed captures folder and the Output folder into a date-and-event parent on the archive drive. Leave the Trash and the Capture One settings behind.

ChronoSync only ever runs between the two sessions drives, whole drive, not one session at a time. The archive drive is filled by hand.

## Export the originals with the TIFFs

Bob Korn works from the DNG, not from your edits, so the finished file and its original should travel together. Capture One does it in one export:

- The TIFF recipe, renamed: output subfolder Edit for Archive TIFF C1.
- A new recipe, made with the plus sign under the recipes list: type Copy original files, output subfolder Edit for Archive Original C1, and Include adjustments unchecked.
- Tick both, select the images, export once. Two subfolders, 1754.tif and 1754.dng.

The originals get the Photo Mechanic copyright pass at the same time as the TIFFs.

## Cleaning up an older session

If little editing was done, start a fresh session. If it holds real work, keep it. Then: rename the session and folders with the date and event, batch rename the captures (replace the Moment prefix, keep the long string as the ID), cull, check exports, copyright the 4 and 5 stars including originals, archive.

## The SD card, every time

Once the card is on two drives and the sizes match: select all on the card, Command Delete, empty the Trash while the card is still in the slot. That is all formatting means for this card. Do it before the next outing so there is never a "what is on this card?" moment.

## Personal photos stay in the library

Space is not the problem on a half terabyte phone. Thin the throwaways on the laptop (select, delete, confirm; it clears the phone too). Tennis video goes to the tennis drive. Family photos can also go to the family drive with File, Export, Export Unmodified Originals into a folder. Shared albums do not move anything.

## The stock camera can shoot RAW

Settings, Camera, Formats already has ProRAW and Resolution Control on. In the Camera app, tap HEIF at the top left and it becomes RAW, 48 MP, one or the other. Old sessions with only HEIC files never had a raw; no point making TIFFs from them.

# The Messages fix

Green messages are SMS and go out through your iPhone, so the phone has to be online and nearby. Blue messages are iMessage over the internet. A blue failure to another iPhone user is more likely a stale connection between the Mac and your Apple ID, which is what the recipe refreshes.

1. On the Mac, open Messages, then Messages, Settings in the top menu.
2. Click the iMessage tab, the @ symbol in the middle of the top row.
3. Click Sign Out.
4. Sign back in with your Apple ID and password, both in 1Password.
5. Send yourself a test and confirm it lands on both devices.

Checked and fine: on the phone, Settings, Apps, Messages, Text Message Forwarding has the MacBook Pro on. The long-gone iPad still listed under "You can be reached at" is harmless.
